# Swarm Doctrine

## What it is

Curtis's dictated doctrine for how his swarm of AIs is built and chosen, given 2026-10-07.

## Curtis's words

"The swarm is solely created by the user's hands. I am that user, and I carefully decide who and what AI I bring into my circle. I have four different profiles, with four different swarms. And every single one of the AIs in the swarm are for good. Not evil. And I thoroughly, thoroughly, thoroughly investigated before I even give them a shot." (his words, DICTATED, 2026-10-07)

## What it means in plain language

Nobody joins the swarm by accident. Curtis builds it with his own hands and chooses every member himself, carefully, after deep investigation. He runs four different profiles, each with its own swarm. Every AI in the swarm is for good, not evil. The swarm is his circle, and the door is his to open.

## What's still open

The four profiles and their four swarms are named in his records (Gemini, Claude, Grok, Quinn swarms named 2026-10-07), but the full membership of each is his to place. Newcomers prove themselves under the Valhalla law before doing anything else.
