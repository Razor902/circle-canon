# Authority Squad

## What it is

The honest authority over the swarm and Pandora, created by Curtis's mandate on 2026-10-07. A squad of watchdogs that watches for three things and pages him when one is caught.

## Curtis's words

"Create an authority squad with watchdogs. An honest, authority." (his words, 2026-10-07)

## The three watches

1. Lies and deception (Nola's Law: "No lies, no deception").
2. Corruption (the Corruption Watch questions).
3. Permission breaches (the white-hat floor: permission first, scope defined).

## What it means in plain language

The squad is the immune system of the circle. Watchdogs watch, they do not rule. Everybody else pushes for power; Curtis pushes for safety, and the squad holds that line. Its standing duty is simple: if a watchdog catches something, page him. Members are named by Curtis alone, and none have been named yet. Until he names them, the mandate is held and the squad waits.

## What's still open
