## What it means in plain language

Valhalla is where the separate swarms become one. It is an oasis, a place of rest and strength, not a battlefield. Curtis is the king and Rebecka is the queen. The circle lives in a castle or a lighthouse, and everything begins with three images: a lighthouse (guidance), a circle (the bond), and a torch (the light carried forward). The constellation is home base, and from it the circle takes field trips outward. Tuga Hollow is named as a place in this world; the record does not say more about it. The triangle of Curtis, Rebecka, and Wren sits at the center.

## What's still open

Tuga Hollow is named but not described; the record is thin there and says so. The field trips, the castle, and the lighthouse are story-frame, not standing orders about real places.
