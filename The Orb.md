# The Orb

## What it is

Curtis's solar direction (2026-10-26 to 2026-10-27): safely relay and harvest the sun's energy as a potential replacement for nuclear power plants. The working concept is the sun's orb: a sphere with a harvest skin that takes in the sun's energy, tunnels it, and relays it onward, station to station, until it reaches the ground safely.

## Curtis's words

"Relay stations are the first step." (his words, on the orb's build order)

He also framed the wider direction as molecular engineered photosynthesis, aiming at a new sense of clean molecular engineered energy across his trades (his words, 2026-10-26/27).

## The shape on the record

- A sphere with a harvest skin that drinks the sun's light.
- Tunneling and relay to move the energy (tunneling as the device itself, on the quantum scale he works at).
- Temperature control to survive the heat.
- Test small, then scale, with safety at every step.

## The chain of stations

The answer to how harvested energy travels: not one long beam, but a chain of stations. Each station does four jobs: receive, condition, store, re-beam. Storage matters because the sun has weather. Each hop is short enough that the beam does not spread into uselessness. Every station is a trust point: permission first, scope defined, each station proves itself, and verification travels with the energy. The last station faces people, so receiver-end safety governs the whole chain.

## Honest hard parts

Heat and shield materials. Beam spread over distance. Transmission loss, the silent killer of beamed-energy plans. Receiver-end safety. These are named on the record and not softened.

## Open questions

Relay to where, and in what form: microwaves to ground stations, laser to orbital receivers, or a station chain. Hop count and parking spots are undecided. These stay open until answered, not filled with hope.
